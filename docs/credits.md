# Credit and references

Kaiwen Jin is the software author; contact `yui.ui.siki@gmail.com`. The initial
integer/tree algorithms were developed for the public
[2026 mechanical-Lie research package](https://github.com/yuiyuiui/AIM/tree/8dc12e3f97d7c2230981ad0d46ab00aa06188bfd/research/solutions/519-euclidean-mechanical-lie-algebra).
This standalone release adds the installable validated public interface,
complete polynomial realizations, worked examples, independent coefficient
checks and computed visualizations. It uses a fresh software history.

The mathematical background is attributable to these primary sources:

1. R. I. McLachlan and A. Murua, *The Lie algebra of classical mechanics*,
   Journal of Computational Dynamics 6(2) (2019), 345–360.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3934/jcd.2019017),
   [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07554).
   Structural graded decomposition, tree representations and finite dimensions.
2. S. Blanes, F. Casas and A. Murua, *Splitting methods for differential
   equations*, Acta Numerica 33 (2024), 1–161.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0962492923000077),
   [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01722).
   Context for mechanical commutators in geometric integration.
3. F. Chapoton and M. Livernet, *Pre-Lie algebras and the rooted trees operad*,
   International Mathematics Research Notices 2001(8) (2001), 395–408.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1155/S1073792801000198).
4. N. Bergeron and J.-L. Loday, *The symmetric operation in a free pre-Lie
   algebra is magmatic*, Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society
   139(5) (2011), 1585–1597.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-2010-10813-4),
   [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.1204).
   Items 3–4 concern the associated manuscript's structural proof, not extra
   runtime algorithms or a software validation certificate.
